Socio-economic status from satellite imagery

Estimated poverty and socio-economic status for every grid cell from satellite imagery with convolutional neural networks, first at 1 × 1 km and then refined to 100 × 100 m.

How it works

InputSatellite tilesOne image per grid cell
ModelCNNLearns visual cues of wealth
EstimateSES per cellSocio-economic score
Refine100 m gridFiner cells, same method
UseMapsTargeting at neighbourhood scale

Roofs, roads and greenery carry information about how well-off an area is; a CNN learns to read them.

Why resolution matters

At 1 km, a rich block and a poor block can average into one middling number. At 100 m the difference shows.
