Digital Personas from Online, Location and Mobility Behaviour

Thirty-plus named personas for 80M+ monthly active subscribers, built from what people do online, where they go and how they move, plus a look-alike model that finds more people like any customer group.

How it works

InputOperator dataTop-ups, calls, online and location behaviour
SequenceBehaviour as tokensEach person's apps and places, in order
EmbedWord2VecPeople with similar habits land close together
ClusterGroupsDense regions in behaviour space
NamePersonasNamed with the business: mom with kids, blue collar…
ExpandLook-alikeFind more people like a seed group

Built in Spark at hundreds of millions of rows. The same trick that makes words with similar meaning sit close together places people with similar routines close together.

Behaviour space: personas are neighbourhoods of people

Word2Vec turns each subscriber into a point; people with similar routines end up near each other, and those clusters become named personas.

Schematic. Positions and cluster sizes are illustrative; real distributions are client-confidential.
